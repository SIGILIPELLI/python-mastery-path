World record journey

Mastery Path has applied to Guinness World Records for a new record title. This page shows exactly where that application stands and how the numbers behind it are measured. No record has been awarded.

Status: Application submitted – awaiting Guinness World Records' assessment. Guinness World Records reviews new-title applications before deciding whether a record title can exist; the standard review time is several weeks. Nothing below 'Application submitted' has happened yet. Mastery Path is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Guinness World Records.

What is Mastery Path?

A free library of 102 structured courses, each in four levels, written and published by Vinod Bhanuja on one website. No account or payment is needed.

Why apply for a record?

To have the size and structure of the library measured by an independent body against published rules, instead of relying on our own claims.

Title applied for

Most free structured online courses created and published by an individual on a single website
Submitted as a new-record-title application on 19 November 2026. Wording is proposed by us; Guinness World Records decides whether a title exists and what its rules are.

How the count is measured

Courses and lessons are counted by the open audit tool under the published quality & verification rules: one course counted once, projects and quizzes never counted as lessons, external links never counted, every lesson URL checked.

Application status

  2. Application submittedNew-record-title application sent on 19 November 2026.
  3. Application under reviewWaiting for Guinness World Records to assess whether the title is distinct and measurable.
  4. Record title approvedOnly if Guinness World Records accepts the title and sends its guidelines.
  5. Record attempt completedEvidence gathered exactly as the official guidelines require.
  6. Officially recognisedOnly after Guinness World Records reviews the evidence and confirms the record in writing.

Evidence collection

Each audit produces dated inventories, a verification report, a duplicate report and an evidence register with SHA-256 checksums. Archived audits are never edited. If Guinness World Records accepts a title, the formal attempt will follow their guidelines exactly, including any independent witnesses or reviewers they require.

Frequently asked questions

Has Mastery Path won a Guinness World Record?

No. An application for a new record title has been submitted. Nothing has been approved or awarded.

Is Mastery Path working with Guinness World Records?

No. Anyone can apply for a record. Mastery Path is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Guinness World Records.

Which courses would count?

Only first-party courses that pass every rule on the Quality & Verification page and have a recorded human review. One course counts once.

Are projects and quizzes counted as lessons?

No. They are counted separately, and the counts are published separately.

Can I check the numbers myself?

Yes. The course and lesson inventories, the verification report and SHA-256 checksums are linked from the Quality & Verification page, and the audit tool is in the public hub repository.

What happens if Guinness World Records says no?

Nothing changes for learners. The courses stay free, and the quality and verification work continues because it makes the library more trustworthy.
