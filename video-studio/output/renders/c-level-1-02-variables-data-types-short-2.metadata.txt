Title: Why adding 1 in C can make your number go negative

Description:
In C, integers have a fixed number of bits, so arithmetic that goes past the type's range wraps around silently instead of raising an error. This Short shows INT_MAX plus one flipping straight to the most negative int, why the compiler won't warn you at runtime, and why a wider or unsigned type only moves the boundary instead of fixing it. Pulled from the C Mastery Path Level 1 lesson on variables, data types and operators.
