Title: C pointers: why +1 doesn't mean +1 byte

Description:
Adding 1 to a pointer in C does not add one byte. The compiler scales it by the size of the pointed-to type, which is exactly why nums[i] equals *(nums + i). The same lesson also explains why arrays are not pointers: pass one into a function and its size collapses from 40 bytes to 8. Watch to see both in action.
