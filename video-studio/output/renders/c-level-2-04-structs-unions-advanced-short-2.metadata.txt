Title: This C struct wastes 4 bytes and it's the field order

Description:
Two C structs with the exact same three fields can have different sizes, purely because of declaration order. This Short shows why sizeof surprises you: the compiler inserts invisible padding bytes so each field lands on an aligned address. Reorder wide members first and the struct shrinks for free. Full walkthrough, plus unions and bit-fields, in the lesson.
