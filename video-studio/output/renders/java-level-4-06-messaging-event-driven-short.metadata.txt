Title: Kafka: One Topic, Two Totally Different Behaviors

Description:
A single Kafka topic can behave like a work queue or a broadcast to every subscriber -- and the switch isn't a special queue type, it's the consumer's group ID. We break down the producer key, the consumer group config, and how Kafka quietly supports both point-to-point and pub/sub messaging on the exact same topic.
