Title: Your await calls are running one at a time (and slowing you down)

Description:
Two async operations that don't depend on each other should not be awaited one after another. Sequential awaits make a 200ms + 200ms job take 400ms; Promise.all fires both immediately and finishes in ~200ms. This Short shows the exact fix from the JavaScript Mastery Path Promises & Async/Await lesson, plus the one catch: Promise.all rejects the moment any single promise fails.
