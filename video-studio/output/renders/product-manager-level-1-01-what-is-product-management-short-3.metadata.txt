Title: A Product Manager's Real Week: One Support Ticket to a Buildable Plan

Description:
What does a product manager actually do all week? We walk through one real Monday-to-Friday on a SaaS product's notifications area: reading support tickets, interviewing a churned user, writing a one-page problem statement, fighting for engineering time, and drafting the spec. Notice what never happens: no code, no final design, no sprint timeline. The PM's job is turning a noisy signal into a defined, prioritized, buildable plan.
