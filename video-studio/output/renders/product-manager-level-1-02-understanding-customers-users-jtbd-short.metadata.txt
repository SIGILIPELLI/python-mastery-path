Title: Why Your Real Competitor Isn't Who You Think

Description:
Two totally different products can be fighting for the exact same customer -- because people don't buy products, they hire them for a job. This is the Jobs-to-be-Done lens from Level 1 of the Product Manager Mastery Path, and it explains why your real competition is often not who you'd assume. The full lesson also covers behavioral customer interviews and building personas the right way.
