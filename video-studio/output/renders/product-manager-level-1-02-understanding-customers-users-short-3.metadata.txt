Title: The Interview Question That Makes Customers Lie to You

Description:
The fastest way to ruin a customer interview is to ask people to predict their own future behavior. "Would you use a feature that does X?" feels productive, but people are unreliable narrators of what they'll do next -- and reliable narrators of what they already did. This Short shows the swap from leading, hypothetical questions to behavioral ones, plus why 4-5 consistent stories are enough to act on.
