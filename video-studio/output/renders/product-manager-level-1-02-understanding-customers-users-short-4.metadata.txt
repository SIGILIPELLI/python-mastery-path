Title: Your user persona is quietly lying to you

Description:
A persona is a composite sketch of a user type built from real research — useful shared shorthand for "who are we building for." But it only ever answers who, never why. This Short shows how interviews, Jobs-to-be-Done, and personas actually fit together, and why teams over-rely on the persona.
