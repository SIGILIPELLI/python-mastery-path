Title: Why Product Teams Ship Features Nobody Uses (The Discovery Fix)

Description:
The fastest way to waste a quarter: a stakeholder says "build X," and two weeks later an engineer is building X, with nobody checking whether X solves a real problem. This Short walks through the discovery workflow that stops solution-jumping: collect signals for a week or two, cluster them, validate the top clusters with interviews, and only then write the problem statement. Includes the analytics-team worked example from the lesson.
