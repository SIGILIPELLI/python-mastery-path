Title: One angry support ticket is not a problem. It's a signal.

Description:
New PMs treat every loud request as a problem to solve, then wonder why they keep shipping fixes for symptoms. This Short breaks down the difference between a signal (one piece of raw evidence) and a problem (the validated pain a cluster of signals points to), using the B2B analytics worked example from Level 1's Problem Discovery lesson: two support tickets, a return-rate drop, and one lost renewal that all cluster into a single real problem. Collect signals, cluster them, then write the problem statement before anyone proposes a solution.
