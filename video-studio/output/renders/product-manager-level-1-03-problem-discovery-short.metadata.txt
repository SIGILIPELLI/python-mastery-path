Title: The One-Sentence PM Template That Fixes Vague Problem Statements

Description:
Most 'problem statements' in a PRD are just vague vibes dressed up as a document -- and vague statements produce scattered, mismatched solutions. Here's the four-part template (who, pain, situation, consequence, plus evidence) that makes two different engineers design roughly the same fix. Pulled from Module 3 of the Product Manager Mastery Path.
