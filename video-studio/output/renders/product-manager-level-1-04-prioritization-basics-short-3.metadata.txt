Title: The RICE math that kills your highest-impact feature

Description:
In this lesson's worked example, SSO support scores the maximum on per-user impact and still loses the priority fight. We walk the actual RICE numbers: how low reach and high effort drag a strong bet below a small, cheap fix that reaches everyone. Then the caveat that matters -- RICE informs the call, it doesn't make it.
