Title: Kill a third of your backlog in 5 minutes, no spreadsheet

Description:
Before you build a RICE spreadsheet, run the fastest prioritization framework there is: a value vs. effort 2x2. Plot every backlog item, then delete the entire low-value, high-effort quadrant with zero further analysis. In the lesson's worked example, a team drops 6 of 20 items that way and only RICE-scores what survives. Rough triage first, precision later.
