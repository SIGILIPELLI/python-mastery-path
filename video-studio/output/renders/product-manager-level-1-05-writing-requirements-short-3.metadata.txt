Title: The PRD Section Everyone Skips (And Regrets Mid-Sprint)

Description:
Most last-minute sprint blowups trace back to one missing paragraph: the non-goals section of the PRD. This Short walks a real mini-PRD and shows why explicitly writing "this will NOT support X" prevents the most common scope-creep argument. A blank non-goals section isn't clean scope, it's a warning sign.
