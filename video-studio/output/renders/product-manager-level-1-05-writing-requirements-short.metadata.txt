Title: Why Your User Story Isn't Actually "Done"

Description:
A user story sounds finished the moment the template is filled in -- but without acceptance criteria, "done" is just a guess. This Short breaks down the Given/When/Then format for acceptance criteria, straight from the Writing Requirements lesson in the Product Manager Mastery Path, and why skipping it is where scope arguments quietly start.
