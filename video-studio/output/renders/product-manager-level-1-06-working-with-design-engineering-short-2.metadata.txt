Title: The Design Review Order Most PMs Get Backwards

Description:
Design reviews go wrong two ways: the PM rubber-stamps everything, or relitigates the problem statement at the mockup stage -- way too late, and it wastes the designer's work. This Short breaks down the fixed order that catches real gaps instead: problem fit, acceptance criteria, edge cases, scope creep -- taste last.
