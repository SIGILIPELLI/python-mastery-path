Title: Why Watching Quarterly Revenue Is a PM Trap

Description:
Most PMs know what a metric is. Fewer know the difference between a metric they can steer with and one that only confirms the past. This Short breaks down leading vs. lagging metrics -- and the common mistake of obsessing over a lagging number week to week, long after the decisions that moved it are already history.
