Title: Why "When Will This Ship?" Is a Trap for PMs

Description:
A sales lead puts you on the spot in an all-hands: "Will SSO be ready by our renewal date?" The wrong answer turns your roadmap into a broken promise. This Short breaks down the Now/Next/Later confidence structure and the exact line a PM uses to answer honestly without over-committing.
