Title: PM Pitfall: Sellers Weren't Churning Over a Bug

Description:
Seven support tickets and a churn-exit interview all pointed at the same 'broken' feature -- but the real cause wasn't a bug, it was an undocumented delay nobody told users about. This is the pitfall hiding inside the sample scenario from the Product Manager Mastery Path's Level 1 capstone project: writing a complete PRD. Before you write a single requirement, your problem statement has to name the real problem -- and it's rarely the one users think they're reporting.
