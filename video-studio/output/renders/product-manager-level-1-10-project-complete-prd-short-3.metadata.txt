Title: Your PRD Isn't Done Until It Passes These 5 Questions

Description:
A PRD isn't finished when you've written every section. It's finished when it survives the same review a design and engineering team would put it through. This Short walks the self-review checklist from the Level 1 capstone project: one build interpretation instead of many, testable acceptance criteria, non-goals specific enough to stop a scope-creep fight, a numeric success metric with a baseline, and a problem statement that stands on its own. Run your draft through it before you call it done.
