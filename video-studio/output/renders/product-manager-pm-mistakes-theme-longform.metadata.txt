Title: 6 Product Manager Mistakes That Quietly Sink Products

Description:
Most products don't fail with a bang — they leak. A PM makes a small, reasonable-sounding call, and six months later the team has shipped something nobody uses. This roundup pulls six anti-patterns straight from the Level 1 Product Manager Mastery Path: asking leading, hypothetical questions in customer interviews; solution-jumping from a single signal to a build; using the wrong prioritization framework for the question in front of you; owning design and engineering's jobs instead of your own; publishing a feature-list roadmap with far-future dates; and spending equal time on every stakeholder. For each one we name the mistake, explain why it does damage, and give the fix from the lesson itself.
