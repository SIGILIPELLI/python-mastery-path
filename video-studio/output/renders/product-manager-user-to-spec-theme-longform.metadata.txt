Title: From User to Spec: How a PM Decides What to Build

Description:
Four Product Manager Mastery Path Level 1 lessons, sequenced as one continuous arc: the craft of turning user understanding into a buildable, well-handed-off spec. We start with what a PM is actually accountable for — the what and the why, outcomes over output — then move into understanding customers through behavioral interviews, Jobs-to-be-Done, and personas that don't calcify. From there we write it down: user stories, Given/When/Then acceptance criteria, and the anatomy of a PRD, including why non-goals earn a section every time. We close on the handoff itself — progressive involvement instead of a document over the wall, running a design review against fixed questions, navigating tradeoffs out loud, and the anti-patterns that quietly erode a team's trust. This is deliberately the other half of the Level 1 material from 'A PM's Full Workflow: From Discovery to Roadmap' — no prioritization, metrics, or roadmapping retread here, just user to spec.
