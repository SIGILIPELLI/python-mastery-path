Title: A PM's Full Workflow: From Discovery to Roadmap

Description:
Five Product Manager Mastery Path lessons, followed as one continuous workflow instead of five separate topics. This video traces a single thread — from spotting a raw signal, through validating it into a real problem, ranking it against everything else on the backlog, defining the metric that proves it worked, putting it on an honest roadmap, and defending that roadmap when a stakeholder pushes back. If you've already watched the Level 1 survey or the PRD deep-dive, this is the version that shows how discovery, prioritization, metrics, roadmapping, and stakeholder communication actually chain together in real PM work.
